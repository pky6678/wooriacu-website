##  **Menstrual Irregularities** 

**Common Symptoms**

* Menstrual flow too little or too heavy  
* Cycles too short or too long  
* Periods lasting too short or too long  
* Severe menstrual pain (dysmenorrhea)  
* Irregular spotting or abnormal bleeding  
* Absence of menstruation (amenorrhea)

**Traditional Korean Medicine (TKM) Perspective**

* Caused by imbalance of **Qi, Blood, Yang energy, Heat, or Stagnation (blood clots/obstruction)**

* Common patterns:

  * **Blood Deficiency** → light periods, short duration, fatigue, pale complexion  
  * **Qi/Yang Deficiency** → delayed cycles, heavy or prolonged bleeding, cold hands & feet, low energy  
  * **Heat/Inflammation** → early cycles, heavy bleeding, irritability, body heat  
  * **Stagnation (Blood/Phlegm)** → severe cramps, clots, lower abdominal pain

**Treatment Approach**

* **Herbal medicine**: Nourish Blood, strengthen Qi/Yang, clear Heat, resolve stagnation

* **Acupuncture & Moxibustion**: Improve circulation, warm the uterus, regulate cycles

* **Lifestyle guidance**:

  * Warm abdomen, avoid cold/raw foods & excessive stress  
  * Maintain balanced diet & moderate exercise  
  * Ensure adequate rest and stress management

 In TKM, a **healthy uterus must be warm, well-nourished, and free of stagnation.**  
 By restoring balance, menstrual irregularities can be improved, supporting overall women’s health and fertility.
